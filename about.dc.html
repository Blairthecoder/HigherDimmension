About Higher Dimension

Following Jesus. Serving People.

Higher Dimension Church exists to help people follow Jesus and live the full life He intended, then put that faith to work for the people around them. That mission has driven everything we do for more than 22 years.

We gather across two campuses and online, with a congregation that reaches far beyond Texas. Wherever you're joining from, there's a place for you and a part for you to play.

And we're a church that shows up. We help redevelop under-resourced communities, meet real economic needs, and love our neighbors, because following Jesus is faith with its sleeves rolled up.

Our Leadership

Meet the People Who Carry the Vision.

Lead Pastor & First Lady

Terrance & Torsha Johnson

Terrance Johnson is a pastor, gifted communicator, and recognized leader in his city. As founder and lead pastor of Higher Dimension Church in Southwest Houston and Katy, he has served as a community pioneer, helping redevelop under-resourced neighborhoods and meet the real economic needs of their people.

A Houston native, Pastor Terrance holds a Bachelor of Arts in Bible and Sociology. Alongside First Lady Torsha, he has spent more than two decades teaching biblical truth for real relationships, the heart behind their book and message, The Answer.

Terrance and Torsha still live in their hometown with their three sons, Terrance, Thailan, and Theophilus, and their daughter, Tirzah.

Executive Pastor

Terrance Johnson II

Son of Lead Pastor Terrance Johnson, Executive Pastor Terrance is following in his father's footsteps, carrying the vision forward and leading his generation to Christ. He helps oversee the teams and ministries that keep Higher Dimension moving across every campus and online.

The Testament People Actually Read

Most people will never open the Old Testament or the New. But they will read your life, the way you love, forgive, work, and show up. For many, that is the only Gospel they will ever encounter.

So we're called to be set apart, not distant or self-righteous, but noticeably different, marked by grace, integrity, and hope in a world that runs short on all three.

That's who we're becoming together: living witnesses to the power of God through Christ, because a changed life is the most convincing sermon anyone will ever read.
