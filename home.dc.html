Meet our senior pastor

Bishop Dr. Apostle Raymond E. Nnolim

Born in West Africa and trained at Rhema Bible College in Tulsa, Bishop Raymond has pastored and planted churches across the United States and carried the Gospel internationally. In 2017 he answered God's call to bring the Holy Fire Ministry to the Dallas area, and today he leads Jesus Embassy Church in Plano.
