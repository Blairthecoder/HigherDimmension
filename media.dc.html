The God of New Beginnings

Our most recent Sunday service, recorded live in Plano.

October 11, 2026 · 75 min

2 Corinthians 5:17Isaiah 43:18-19Lamentations 3:22-23

Preached on the church’s first Sunday back in its renovated building. Bishop Raymond reminds the church that God specializes in fresh starts and is not finished with anyone.

Notes from the message

  • Release the past. People who stay on yesterday miss the opportunities of today, and you can’t drive forward while looking in the rearview mirror.
  • Recognize what God is doing. Ask God to open your spiritual eyes to the new thing He is doing now, even when your surroundings feel dry.
  • Be renewed in Christ. A true new beginning is a recreation by the Spirit, so you are no longer known by past failures.

The Power of Unity

John 17:20-21Ephesians 4:3-6Acts 2:1

Jesus prayed that all who believe would be one. Bishop Raymond teaches that unity is not uniformity, but oneness of heart that reflects the nature of God.

Notes from the message
  • Unity reflects God, who is one and undivided.
  • Unity protects believers, like a flock of sheep.
  • Unity brings power and blessing, as when the believers were together in one place at Pentecost.
  • Keeping unity takes effort: “make every effort to keep the oneness of the Spirit in the bond of peace.”

The Goodness of God

Psalm 34:1-8

God is good because of who He is, not because of what He does in our circumstances. Bishop Raymond encourages the church to choose to believe that God is good, even before every prayer is answered.

Notes from the message
  • God’s goodness is part of His nature, so it does not depend on our circumstances.
  • It is present every day, offering mercy and grace through life’s challenges.

Living by Faith

Hebrews 11:1Hebrews 11:6James 2

Faith is the title deed to what God has promised. Bishop Raymond explains what faith is, and why believing has to be joined with action.

Notes from the message
