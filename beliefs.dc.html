01

The Scriptures inspired

The Bible is the inspired Word of God, a living revelation that is infallible and enduring. It is the authoritative standard for what we believe and how we live, above conscience and reason.

2 Timothy 3:15–171 Peter 1:23–25Hebrews 4:12
02

The one true Godhead

God is eternal and self-existent, revealed as three distinct persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, who are one. Jesus Christ is God manifested in the flesh, co-equal and co-eternal with the Father and the Holy Spirit.

John 1:1–5, 10–14Matthew 28:18–20
03

Man, his fall and redemption

God created humanity good and upright, but through deliberate disobedience mankind fell. Our only hope of redemption is in Jesus Christ, the Son of God.

Genesis 1:26–31Genesis 3:1–7Romans 5:12–21
04

The salvation of man

  • The way of salvation. Redemption comes only through the shed blood of Jesus. His work on the cross provides both salvation and divine healing. We receive it by grace through faith, confessing Jesus as Lord and believing God raised Him from the dead.
  • The evidence of salvation. The Spirit bears inward witness that we are God's children, and a righteous, holy life is the outward evidence others can see.
  • Faith and works. We are saved by faith, not works, yet our works will determine our eternal rewards.
Romans 3:24John 3:3Psalm 103:3Ephesians 2:8–10Romans 10:8–10Romans 8:161 John 3:18–19, 24Matthew 7:16–20Galatians 2:162 Corinthians 5:10
05

Baptism in water

Everyone who has truly repented and believed should be baptized by immersion. Baptism declares to the world that we have died with Christ to the old life and been raised to walk in newness of life.

Matthew 28:19Romans 6:4Acts 10:47–48
06

The Lord's Supper

Believers share the Lord's Supper in remembrance of Christ's body and blood, each one examining themselves before partaking. At Jesus Embassy, communion is shared every Saturday during Morning Glow prayer.

1 Corinthians 11:24–25, 28
07

The promise of the Father

Every believer is entitled to, and should earnestly seek, the baptism in the Holy Spirit. This experience follows the new birth and brings power for life and service along with the gifts of the Spirit.

Luke 24:49Acts 1:4–5, 8Acts 2:38Acts 10:44–46Acts 11:14–161 Corinthians 12:1–31
08

The evidence of the baptism in the Holy Spirit

The initial physical sign of the baptism in the Holy Spirit is speaking with other tongues as the Spirit gives utterance. It continues to show itself in power for public testimony and service.

Acts 2:4Acts 10:44–46Acts 19:2, 6Acts 1:8Galatians 5:16–26
09

The Church

The Church is the body of Christ and the habitation of God through the Spirit. Every born-again believer is part of it, with their name written in heaven.

Ephesians 1:22–23Ephesians 2:19–22Hebrews 12:23
10

Total prosperity

God's blessing reaches every part of life:

  • Spiritual: new life and reconciliation with God in Christ (3 John 2; 2 Corinthians 5:17–21).
  • Mental: a sound mind, a renewed way of thinking, and perfect peace (2 Timothy 1:7; Romans 12:2; Isaiah 26:3).
  • Physical: healing through the suffering of Christ (Isaiah 53:4–5; Matthew 8:17; 1 Peter 2:24).
  • Financial: blessing that follows faithful giving (Malachi 3:10–11; Luke 6:38; 2 Corinthians 9:6–15; Deuteronomy 28:1–14).
  • Social: favor with God and people (Proverbs 3:4; Luke 2:52; Esther 2:15).
11

The blessed hope

Jesus will return to gather His saints to Himself, and we will be with the Lord forever.

1 Corinthians 15:51–521 Thessalonians 4:16–17
12

The lake of fire

Those who reject Christ's redemptive work will be eternally separated from God. The devil, his angels, the beast, the false prophet, and all whose names are not found in the Book of Life will face everlasting punishment in the lake of fire, the second death.

Revelation 19:20Revelation 20:10–15
13

The millennial reign of Jesus

Jesus will return with His saints to reign on the earth for a thousand years, followed by a new heaven and a new earth.

2 Thessalonians 1:7Revelation 19:11–16Revelation 20:1–6Revelation 21
